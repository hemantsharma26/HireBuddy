🛠 Solution Layer 1 — Minimum Price Guard (Must Have)

Har task me:

Minimum acceptable price range

Example:

User post:
Movie companion – ₹800

System:

Min bid allowed: ₹600

Max bid: ₹1200

Matlab:
Free fall nahi hoga.

🛠 Solution Layer 2 — Smart Bid Positioning

Lowest bid = winner nahi hona chahiye ❌

Show ranking like:

⭐ Best Value (AI pick)
💰 Lowest Price
🏆 Top Rated
⚡ Fastest Available


Uber bhi cheapest driver nahi deta.

🛠 Solution Layer 3 — Trust Weighted Bidding (Very Powerful)

Sab bids equal treat mat karo.

Formula:
Bid Score = Price + Rating + Trust + Completion %

To:
₹700 trusted banda > ₹500 unknown banda

🛠 Solution Layer 4 — Suggested Pricing Engine

Isse:
